# Settings: Fight Reports

How each individual fight's Discord post looks and which sections it includes.

## What's on this page

### Appearance

- **Guild icon** — an image shown on the posts (path box plus **Browse...**). The default is SparkyBot's WvW icon.
- **Embed color** — the accent color of the Discord post. Click the color swatch to pick a new one; the hex code next to it shows the current choice.

### Sections of the Discord fight report

Thirteen tick boxes decide which stat sections appear in each fight post, grouped in three columns:

**Summary**
- **Show Quick Report** — the at-a-glance headline of the fight.
- **Show Downs/Kills** — downed and killed counts.
- **Show Enemy Breakdown** — what the enemy squad was made of, by profession.
- **Show Top Enemy Skills** — which enemy skills hurt your squad most.

**Offense**
- **Show Damage Stats** — top damage dealers.
- **Show Burst Damage** — biggest short-window damage spikes.
- **Show Crowd Control** — crowd-control (stuns, knockdowns, etc.) leaders.
- **Show Strips** — boon strips (removing enemy buffs).
- **Show Offensive Boons** — attack-boosting buffs your squad kept up.

**Support & Defense**
- **Show Heals** — top healers.
- **Show Cleanses** — condition cleanses (removing harmful effects from allies).
- **Show Defense** — damage taken / tanking stats.
- **Show Defensive Boons** — protective buffs your squad kept up.

Untick anything your guild doesn't care about — posts get shorter and easier to read.

## Common problems

- **Posts feel like a wall of stats** — untick sections here; **Show Quick Report** plus one or two favorites makes a compact post.
- **Icon doesn't show** — check the path points at a real image file; use **Browse...** rather than typing it.
- **Color didn't change** — the new color applies to posts made after you click **OK**/**Apply**.
