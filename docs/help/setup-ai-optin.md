# Setup: Want AI commentary?

The second screen of the setup wizard. It asks one simple question: do you want SparkyBot to add an AI hype-commentator blurb to each fight report, and optionally read it aloud in a voice you pick?

Everything else — fight reports, fight summaries, Discord posting — is complete without AI. Saying no here keeps the rest of the app exactly as capable, just simpler.

## What's on this screen

- **No thanks — keep it simple** — the default. The wizard skips every AI and voice screen, and the finished app shows no AI options anywhere.
- **Yes — set up AI commentary** — two extra screens are added to the wizard: [AI Fight Commentary](setup-ai-commentary.md) and [Voice / Text-to-Speech](setup-voice.md). AI commentary needs an AI provider; free local options work.

A note on the screen reminds you: you can change your mind any time in **Settings → Application** (the **AI features** switch).

## Common problems

- **You said no but want AI later** — open **Settings → Application** and tick **Enable AI features (fight commentary and voice)**. The AI pages appear in Settings immediately after you save.
- **You said yes but changed your mind** — untick the same switch in **Settings → Application**. Every AI control disappears; nothing else is affected.
